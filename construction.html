Construction

Complete MEPFS works delivered as planned, multi-phase construction projects — installed, tested, commissioned and documented to the specification you signed off.

What this covers

The long projects

Construction is our long-duration track. These are programmes measured in weeks and months rather than hours: new builds, fit-outs, expansions and whole-system replacements where the building services have to be designed into the fabric of the project rather than bolted on at the end.

We take MEPFS packages — Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire protection and Sanitary — and run them as one coordinated scope. That means a single set of coordinated services drawings, one procurement schedule, one site team answering for the lot, and no gaps between trades for problems to fall into.

Typical project profile

Commercial fit-outs, office towers, warehouses and light industrial facilities, schools and institutional buildings, retail developments and data-adjacent spaces requiring certified cabling.

How we are engaged

As the MEPFS subcontractor to a main contractor, or directly by the building owner or developer for a services-only package.

Core installations

What we install

Highlighted scope

Structured Cabling

End-to-end installation of the physical network layer, built and certified so that it outlives several generations of the equipment plugged into it.

  • Copper horizontal cabling — Cat 6, Cat 6A and above
  • Fibre optic backbones, splicing and termination
  • Comms rooms, racks, cabinets and patch panels
  • Cable containment, tray, trunking and conduit routes
  • Labelling, record drawings and certified test results
  • Pathways coordinated with power, HVAC and fire services
Highlighted scope

Fire Protection

Life-safety systems installed to the approved design and the governing code, then witnessed, tested and signed off — the part of the building nobody can afford to have almost finished.

  • Automatic sprinkler systems and wet risers
  • Fire hydrants, hose reels and landing valves
  • Fire pumps, jockey pumps and pump room pipework
  • Detection and alarm — detectors, panels, sounders
  • Suppression systems for plant and server rooms
  • Fire-stopping of service penetrations and compartments
Full scope

The complete MEPFS package

Structured cabling and fire protection sit inside a wider services scope. Here is everything the construction track can carry.

M

Mechanical

Air and water systems that hold the building at its design conditions.

  • HVAC plant — chillers, AHUs, FCUs, split and VRF systems
  • Ductwork fabrication, installation, insulation and balancing
  • Chilled water, condenser water and refrigerant pipework
  • Ventilation, extract, pressurisation and smoke management
E

Electrical

Power from the incomer to the final socket, plus the low-voltage systems that ride alongside it.

  • Main and sub-main distribution, switchgear and distribution boards
  • Small power, lighting, emergency lighting and controls
  • Containment, earthing, bonding and lightning protection
  • Generators, UPS and changeover arrangements
  • Structured cabling and other extra-low-voltage systems
P

Plumbing

Water in, water out, at the pressure and flow the building was designed for.

  • Cold and hot water supply, storage tanks and booster pump sets
  • Soil, waste and vent pipework
  • Storm water, roof drainage and sump arrangements
  • Pressure testing, flushing, chlorination and water quality sign-off
F

Fire Protection

Detection, suppression and containment, installed as a coordinated life-safety system.

  • Sprinkler, hydrant and hose reel systems
  • Fire pump rooms and associated pipework
  • Fire detection, alarm and public address interfaces
  • Gas and clean-agent suppression for critical rooms
  • Passive fire protection and fire-stopping
S

Sanitary

Fixtures, fittings and the drainage infrastructure that serves them.

  • Sanitary ware, fittings and accessories
  • Waste and vent stacks, floor drains and traps
  • Grease interceptors and drainage pre-treatment
  • Water treatment and filtration connections
Project delivery

How a construction project runs

Long projects fail in the gaps between stages. These are the stages we hold, and what you receive at each one.

Tender & scope review

Drawings and specifications reviewed, quantities taken off, clarifications raised before pricing.

Shop drawings & approvals

Coordinated services drawings, material submittals and authority approvals secured.

Procurement & mobilisation

Long-lead items ordered against the programme; site team, tools and permits in place.

Installation

Phased installation under supervision, with daily reporting and weekly progress against programme.

Testing & commissioning

Pressure tests, cable certification, system commissioning and witnessed demonstrations.

Authority inspection

Attendance at inspections, closing out observations and obtaining the required sign-offs.

Handover

As-built drawings, O&M manuals, test certificates, warranties and operator training.

Defects & aftercare

Defects liability support, then a seamless move onto a maintenance contract if you want one.

At handover

O&M manuals

Questions

Before you brief us

What do you need from us to produce a quotation?

Architectural and services drawings, the specification, and a bill of quantities if one exists. If the design is not finished, a scope description and a site visit are enough for a budget estimate, which we then firm up once drawings are issued.

Can you take only part of the MEPFS scope?

Yes. Plenty of our work is a single package — structured cabling alone, or fire protection alone. Taking the full scope removes coordination risk, but it is not a condition of working with us.

Do you work as a subcontractor to a main contractor?

Routinely. We are comfortable working inside a main contractor's programme, permit system and reporting cadence, and attending coordination meetings as the services subcontractor.

Who handles authority approvals and inspections?

We prepare the submittals for our scope and attend inspections. Where the main contractor or consultant owns the overall submission, we provide everything their package needs on their timetable.

What happens after the project finishes?

Defects liability support runs for the agreed period. Most clients then move the building onto a maintenance contract under our engineering services track, so the team that installed the system keeps looking after it.

Have a project to price?

Send drawings and a specification, or just describe the building and the scope. We will come back with questions first and a number second.
