What we install

Life-safety systems installed to the approved design and the governing code, then tested, witnessed and signed off.

Sprinklers, fire pumps and the FDAS go in as one package from our Tanauan office, with free consultancy on BFP, DOLE and PEZA permits so approvals do not hold up your programme. The engineer in charge is a licensed Mechanical Engineer, Master Plumber and Registered Master Electrician.

Sprinklers and water systems

  • Automatic fire sprinkler systems
  • Wet standpipes, hose reels and landing valves
  • Fire hydrants and yard mains
  • Fire pumps, jockey pumps and pump rooms

Fire detection and alarm (FDAS)

  • Smoke, heat and gas detection
  • Manual call points, sounders and strobes
  • FDAS control panels and loop wiring

Permits and upkeep

Questions from Batangas clients

Which parts of Batangas do you cover?

All of Batangas. We are based in Tanauan and regularly work in Sto. Tomas, Malvar, Lipa and the FPIP, LISP III and LIMA industrial parks, and travel to Batangas City, Calaca, Nasugbu and the rest of the province. We also take projects in Laguna, Cavite and nationwide.

Do you help with BFP, DOLE and PEZA requirements?

Yes. We give free consultancy on BFP, DOLE and PEZA permits and prepare the test records and as-built drawings that inspectors ask for.

Can you maintain or repair an existing fire system?

Yes. We take on preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance of FDAS and sprinkler systems, and repairs raised by inspection findings, as single visits or annual service contracts.

Can you work while the plant is running?

Yes. We phase the work by area and schedule tie-ins and shutdowns of the fire main around your production hours.
