About us

Axia Engineering Services

A building services contractor built around two capabilities that most companies keep apart: constructing MEPFS systems, and keeping them working once the scaffolding comes down.

Who we are

Two tracks, one team

Most building services firms pick a side. Project contractors install and leave. Maintenance firms arrive years later to a building they have never seen, holding drawings that stopped being accurate at handover.

Axia runs both. Our construction track delivers long-duration MEPFS projects — structured cabling, fire protection, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and sanitary works. Our engineering services track handles the short works: repairs, call-outs and planned maintenance.

The two feed each other. Installing systems keeps our maintenance teams current with how modern plant is actually built. Maintaining systems keeps our project teams honest about what is serviceable five years in — which valve nobody can reach, which panel was mounted where no ladder fits.

How we operate

What we hold ourselves to

Safety is not a line item

Risk assessments and method statements before work starts, permits where required, and the authority for anyone on site to stop a job that has become unsafe.

Write it down

Scope, variations, test results and service findings go in writing. If it was not recorded, it cannot be relied on later — by you or by us.

Fix the cause

Replacing the part that failed is the easy half. We look for why it failed, and tell you even when the answer is inconvenient.

Programmes we can hold

We quote dates we believe, and raise early warning the moment something upstream puts them at risk. A late warning costs more than a late date.

Materials as specified

Approved, traceable components. Where a substitution genuinely makes sense we propose it for approval — we never quietly swap it in.

Reachable people

You deal with the people doing the work, not a call centre layer between you and the engineer who knows your building.
