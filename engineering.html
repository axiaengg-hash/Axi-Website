Engineering Services

Repairs and maintenance. Short, focused interventions that return a building system to specification quickly — and planned servicing that stops the next failure from happening at all.

What this covers

The short works

Engineering services is our short-duration track: work measured in hours and days rather than months. A pump that has stopped. A sprinkler head that needs replacing after an inspection. A floor of data outlets that went dead overnight. A quarterly service visit that keeps all three from happening.

The engineering is the same as on our construction projects. What changes is the clock. These jobs are scoped fast, attended fast, and closed out with a report that tells you what failed, what was done, and what will fail next if it is left alone.

Reactive — something has broken

Call-out, diagnosis and repair. We attend, find the root cause rather than the symptom, and get the system back in service.

Planned — before it breaks

Scheduled preventive maintenance on a contract, with a visit calendar, task sheets and asset records you can audit.

Two ways to use us

Repairs and maintenance

Reactive

Repairs

Something has failed and the building is working around it. We attend, diagnose properly, and fix it — temporary make-safe first where safety demands it, permanent repair as soon as parts allow.

  • Emergency and out-of-hours call-outs
  • Fault diagnosis and root-cause investigation
  • Component replacement and like-for-like repair
  • Make-safe and temporary measures where needed
  • Leak detection and pipework repairs
  • Electrical fault-finding and rectification
  • Cabling fault-finding, re-termination and re-certification
  • Written report with cause, action taken and recommendations
Planned

Maintenance

Scheduled servicing on a calendar you agree in advance, carried out against a task list per asset. The cheapest repair is the one the service visit prevented.

  • Preventive maintenance visits — monthly, quarterly or annual
  • Fire protection inspection, testing and servicing
  • HVAC servicing, filter changes and performance checks
  • Pump, tank and water system servicing
  • Electrical inspection, thermal checks and board tightening
  • Asset registers and maintenance history records
  • Condition reporting with prioritised remedial recommendations
  • Spares planning for critical components
Coverage

What we repair and maintain

The same five MEPFS disciplines we install, we also service — including systems we did not install ourselves.

Mechanical & HVAC

Air conditioning not holding temperature, noisy or vibrating plant, blocked filters, failed fans, refrigerant leaks, poor airflow and balancing drift.

Electrical

Nuisance tripping, failed circuits, overheating terminations, lighting and emergency lighting faults, board upgrades and additional circuits.

Plumbing

Leaks, low pressure, booster pump failures, blocked drainage, tank cleaning, valve replacement and water quality issues.

Fire Protection

Scheduled inspection and testing, sprinkler head replacement, pump testing, panel faults, closing out insurer and authority observations.

Sanitary

Fixture repairs and replacement, blocked waste stacks, trap and seal issues, grease interceptor servicing.

Structured Cabling

Dead outlets, failed links, re-termination, patch management, moves and changes, rack tidy-ups and re-certification.

Short works only help if they are genuinely short. This is how we keep them that way.

Call or email us

Describe the fault and the site. We ask enough questions to send the right trade with the right parts.

Attend & diagnose

We attend within the agreed window, make safe if needed, and identify the actual cause.

Ongoing cover

Service contracts

Most clients start with a repair and end up on a contract. An annual agreement fixes your rates, guarantees your response window, and puts the maintenance calendar in writing.

Guaranteed response

An agreed attendance window for standard and emergency call-outs, written into the contract rather than promised verbally.

Planned visit calendar

Service dates set for the year ahead, scheduled around your operating hours and shutdown periods.

Fixed labour rates

Rates locked for the contract term, so budgeting does not depend on how bad the breakdown was.

Asset register

Every serviceable asset listed with location, make, model, serial and service history — yours to keep.

Compliance support

Inspection and test records kept current for insurers, auditors and the fire authority.

Critical spares

An agreed list of parts held or pre-sourced, so a failure does not become a procurement exercise.

Questions

Before you call us out

Will you service systems your team did not install?

Yes — that is most of this work. We survey the installation first so we are maintaining what is actually there, and flag anything that falls short of the current code before we take it on.

How quickly can you attend an emergency?

It depends on the site and the time of day. Contract clients get a written response window; for everyone else we will tell you honestly when we can be there when you call, rather than promising a time we cannot hold.

How are short works priced?

Call-out plus labour rate plus parts for reactive work, or a fixed annual fee for planned maintenance under a contract. Anything that grows beyond a simple repair is quoted in writing before we proceed.

Can you work outside business hours?

Yes. Much of this work is deliberately scheduled for evenings, weekends or shutdown periods so that the building keeps operating while we are in it.

What if the repair turns out to be a bigger job?

We stop, make safe, and come back to you with the finding and a quotation. If the right answer is a replacement rather than a repair, we will say so — and that work moves onto our construction track.

Something needs fixing?

Describe the fault and the site and we will tell you what it is likely to be, what it will take, and when we can get there.
