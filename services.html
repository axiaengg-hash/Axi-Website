What we install

Four services, installed, tested and then serviced by the same crew.

Electromechanical

For manufacturing plants: installation of production machines and equipment, and the compressed air and cooling water that keep them running.

We fabricate, install and pressure-test the process and utility piping from the compressor room or cooling tower to the last machine on the line, and bring power to the equipment it serves, routed so nothing blocks a production aisle or a maintenance access.

Production machines

  • Installation of production machines and equipment
  • Air, cooling water and power hook-up at each machine
  • Process piping across the production floor
  • Exhaust ducting and rooftop scrubbers

Compressed air

  • Compressor rooms and repiping
  • Air receivers and dryers
  • Distribution headers and drops
  • Pressure testing and leak checks

Cooling water

  • Cooling towers on steel or slab
  • Pump stations and pump rooms
  • Chilled water and process cooling loops
  • Flushing and commissioning

CCTV and structured cabling

For warehouses: cameras placed for what has to be seen, and the network your scanners, printers and systems depend on.

We agree the camera views before anything goes up, mount at height from scaffold or lift, and run cameras and outlets on cabling we label and test link by link, so your IT team can trace any cable in minutes.

CCTV

  • Docks, aisles, gates, yard and perimeter
  • IP cameras, pole and truss mounted
  • PoE switching and NVR storage
  • Guardhouse and office monitoring

Structured cabling

  • Cat6 horizontal cabling
  • Fibre between office and floor
  • Wi-Fi access points for handheld scanners
  • Dock stations and label printers

Racks and records

  • Server racks and wall cabinets
  • Patch panels and cable management
  • Labelling to the as-built drawings
  • Test results for every link

Fire protection and FDAS

Life-safety systems installed to the approved design and the governing code, then witnessed, tested and signed off.

Sprinklers, fire pumps and the fire detection and alarm system go in as one package, with free consultancy on BFP, DOLE and PEZA permits so approvals do not hold up the programme.

Water-based systems

  • Automatic sprinkler systems and wet risers
  • Fire hydrants, hose reels and landing valves
  • Fire pumps, jockey pumps and pump room pipework
  • Suppression for plant and server rooms

Detection and alarm

  • Smoke and heat detectors
  • Manual call points, sounders and strobes
  • FDAS control panels
  • Asenware, Notifier by Honeywell, Hochiki, Edwards, Simplex, Eaton

Approvals and upkeep

  • BFP, DOLE and PEZA permit support
  • Testing and witnessed commissioning
  • Fire-stopping of service penetrations
  • Inspection and servicing

Complete kitchen requirements

The hood, the gas line, gas leak detection and fire suppression, installed as one coordinated system.

The gas detectors and the suppression system both cut the gas supply automatically, so the four parts work together instead of arriving as four separate contracts. Suppression systems we install include Lehavot.

Hood and exhaust

  • Stainless kitchen hoods
  • Exhaust ducting through the roof
  • Exhaust fans

Gas

  • LPG gas line to every appliance
  • Regulators, gauges and shut-off valves
  • Gas leak detectors with alarms and automatic shut-off

Fire suppression

  • Wet-chemical systems over hoods, ducts and appliances
  • Manual pull stations
  • Pressure testing, commissioning and handover

Repairs and upkeep

Not every job is a new installation. We take on breakdowns, inspection findings, planned maintenance and annual service contracts for all four services.

Questions we get

Do you work outside Batangas?

Yes. We are based in Tanauan, Batangas and work nationwide. Recent sites include Calamba, Malvar, Sto. Tomas, Mandaue City and Davao City.

Can one contract cover more than one service?

Yes. A plant can have its machine utilities, fire protection and canteen kitchen under one quotation, one programme and one handover pack.

What do you need to give a quotation?

The layout or drawings if you have them, the machine or equipment list for plant work, and any specification. Without drawings we can survey the site and give a budget figure, then firm it up.

Can you work while the plant or warehouse is operating?

Yes. We phase the work by area and schedule tie-ins and work at height around your production and loading hours.

What do we get at handover?

Test records for pipework, fire systems and cable links, as-built drawings, equipment lists and a walk-through for your team.

Do you help with permits?

Yes. We offer free consultancy on BFP, DOLE and PEZA building and regulatory permits, so approvals do not hold up the programme.
