What we install

The network your scanners, printers, cameras and systems depend on, installed so your IT team can trace any cable in minutes.

We run cabling at height from scaffold or lift, terminate and label it to the as-built drawings, and hand over test results for every link. CCTV views are agreed with you before any camera goes up.

Structured cabling

  • Cat6 and Cat6A horizontal cabling
  • Fibre backbone between buildings and floors
  • Wi-Fi access points for handheld scanners
  • Data outlets, dock stations and label printers

Racks and records

  • Server racks and wall cabinets
  • Patch panels and cable management
  • Labelling to the as-built drawings
  • Test results for every link

CCTV

  • IP cameras for docks, aisles, gates and perimeter
  • Pole and truss mounting at height
  • PoE switching and NVR storage
  • Guardhouse and office monitoring

Questions from Batangas clients

Which parts of Batangas do you cover?

All of Batangas. We are based in Tanauan and regularly work in Sto. Tomas, Malvar, Lipa and the FPIP, LISP III and LIMA industrial parks, and travel to Batangas City, Calaca, Nasugbu and the rest of the province. We also take projects in Laguna, Cavite and nationwide.

Do you test and label the cabling?

Yes. Every link is labelled to the as-built drawings and tested, and the test results are part of the handover pack.

Can you install CCTV and cabling in one contract?

Yes. Cameras, access points and data outlets run on the same structured cabling, under one quotation and one handover.

Can you work in an operating warehouse?

Yes. We phase the work by area and schedule work at height around your loading and picking hours.
