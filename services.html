What we install

Three systems for warehouses and distribution centres: electromechanical, CCTV and structured cabling. Installed, tested, then serviced by the same crew.

Request a quote →

Electromechanical

Power from the incoming supply to every dock, light and machine, and the mechanical services that keep plant running.

We plan the electrical and mechanical works together, so cable tray, pipe racks and lighting share the roof space instead of fighting for it, and nothing blocks a racking bay or a forklift route.

Power and lighting

  • Main and sub-distribution panelboards
  • Feeders, cable tray and conduit
  • High-bay LED lighting over racking
  • Emergency and exit lighting
  • Perimeter and yard lighting, solar included
  • Earthing and bonding

Docks and equipment

  • Roller shutter and dock leveller power
  • Forklift charging bays
  • Generator and ATS connection
  • Power to conveyors and machines
  • Office and mezzanine fit-out wiring

Mechanical services

  • Exhaust fans and ventilation
  • Chilled water and cooling towers
  • Compressed-air lines and compressor rooms
  • Pump stations and process piping
  • Rooftop exhaust and scrubber ducting

CCTV

Cameras placed for what has to be seen: plates at the gate, faces at the dock, every aisle end, the yard at night.

We agree the views before a single camera goes up, mount at height from scaffold or lift, and run each camera on structured cabling we have tested, so the picture you get on day one is the one you get a year later.

Coverage

  • Loading docks and dock doors
  • Racking aisles and aisle ends
  • Gates, guardhouse and yard
  • Perimeter fence lines
  • Cash, cage and high-value areas

System

  • IP cameras, fixed and PTZ
  • PoE switching and camera cabling
  • NVR with storage sized to your retention
  • Monitoring at the guardhouse or office
  • Remote viewing set-up

After handover

  • Fault-finding and camera replacement
  • Repositioning when the layout changes
  • Adding cameras to an existing system
  • Planned cleaning and health checks

Structured cabling

The network your scanners, printers and warehouse system depend on, run neatly, labelled and tested link by link.

Every outlet and patch port is labelled to match the drawings we hand over, and every link is tested before we leave, so your IT team can trace any cable in minutes.

Cabling

  • Cat6 and Cat6A horizontal cabling
  • Fibre backbone between office and floor
  • Outdoor and long-run links to the guardhouse
  • Containment over racking and docks

Outlets on the floor

  • Wireless access points for handheld scanners
  • Dock stations and label printers
  • Offices, mezzanines and meeting rooms
  • Time-clock and access-control points

Racks and records

  • Server racks and wall cabinets
  • Patch panels and cable management
  • Labelling to the as-built drawings
  • Link test results for every outlet

Repairs and upkeep

Not every job is a new build. We take on breakdowns, inspection findings, planned maintenance and annual service contracts for all three systems.

Questions we get

Do you work outside Batangas?

Yes. We are based in Tanauan, Batangas and work nationwide. Recent sites include Calamba, Malvar, Sto. Tomas, Mandaue City and Davao City.

Can you do all three systems on one contract?

That is what we are set up for. One quotation, one programme and one handover pack covering power, cameras and cabling, with one team accountable for how they fit together.

What do you need to give a quotation?

The floor plan and racking layout if you have them, plus any specification. Without drawings we can survey the site and give a budget figure, then firm it up.

Can you work while the warehouse is operating?

Yes. We phase the work by zone and schedule high-level work around your picking and loading hours, including nights and Sundays where needed.

What do we get at handover?

Test results for circuits and cable links, as-built drawings with outlet and camera labels, equipment lists and a walk-through for your team.

Do you help with permits?

We prepare the electrical documents and advise on BFP, DOLE and PEZA requirements so approvals do not hold up the programme.
