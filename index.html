CCTV and cabling

For warehouses: cameras on every dock, aisle and gate, and the network your scanners depend on, tested link by link.

  • IP cameras at docks, aisles and perimeter
  • NVR, storage and monitoring
  • Cat6 cabling and fibre backbone
  • Wi-Fi access points for scanners
  • Racks, cabinets and labelled patching
  • Tested links and as-built records

Fire protection and FDAS

Life-safety systems installed to the approved design and the code, then tested and signed off.

  • Automatic sprinkler systems
  • Fire pumps, jockey pumps and pump rooms
  • Hydrants, hose reels and landing valves
  • Detectors, call points, sounders and panels
  • Suppression for special areas
  • BFP permit support

Complete kitchens

Everything a commercial or canteen kitchen needs to cook safely, installed as one coordinated system.

  • Kitchen hoods, exhaust ducting and fans
  • LPG gas line with regulators and valves
  • Gas leak detectors with automatic shut-off
  • Wet-chemical fire suppression
  • Pressure testing and commissioning
