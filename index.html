One contractor, three systems

A warehouse runs on three systems nobody notices until they stop.

The power behind the docks, lights and plant; the cameras on every bay and gate; the network that carries every scan. We install all three under one contract, planned around the same racking layout, run in shared containment and handed over on the same day.

Inside your warehouse

Choose a system to see where it runs, from the dock door to the server cabinet.

Electromechanical

Power from the incoming supply to every dock, light and machine, and the mechanical services that keep plant running.

  • Panelboards, sub-feeders and cable tray
  • High-bay LED and emergency lighting
  • Dock shutters, levellers and charging bays
  • Generator and ATS connection
  • Exhaust fans and ventilation
  • Chilled water, cooling towers and compressed air
Electromechanical in detail

CCTV

Cameras placed for what has to be seen: plates at the gate, faces at the dock, every aisle end, the yard at night.

  • IP cameras at docks, aisles and perimeter
  • Pole and truss mounting at height
  • PoE switching on its own cabling
  • NVR, storage sized to your retention
  • Guardhouse and office monitoring
  • Servicing, repositioning and repairs
CCTV in detail

Structured cabling

The network your scanners, printers and systems depend on, run neatly, labelled and tested link by link.

  • Cat6 and Cat6A to every outlet
  • Fibre between office and floor
  • Access-point drops for handheld scanners
  • Racks, cabinets and patching
  • Labelling and as-built records
  • Tested and certified links

How a job runs

Survey

We walk the floor with your racking and dock plan, and price against what is really there.

Install

Power, cameras and cable go in on one programme, sequenced around your racking and fit-out.

Test

Circuits tested, every cable link certified, every camera view checked against the agreed coverage.

Maintain

The crew that installed it services it: call-outs, planned maintenance and moves when the layout changes.
