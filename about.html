Axia Engineering Services

A building services contractor built around two capabilities that most companies keep apart: constructing MEPFS systems, and keeping them working once the scaffolding comes down.

Who we are

Two tracks, one team

Established in 2024, Axia Engineering Services is a service company that provides clients with reliable assistance on their engineering and technical needs — from fire protection and mechanical works to electrical, civil and structured cabling projects nationwide.

Most building services firms pick a side. Project contractors install and leave. Maintenance firms arrive years later to a building they have never seen, holding drawings that stopped being accurate at handover.

Axia runs both. Our construction track delivers long-duration MEPFS projects — structured cabling, fire protection, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and complete kitchen systems. Our engineering services track handles the short works: repairs, call-outs and planned maintenance.

The two feed each other. Installing systems keeps our maintenance teams current with how modern plant is actually built. Maintaining systems keeps our project teams honest about what is serviceable five years in — which valve nobody can reach, which panel was mounted where no ladder fits.

Our philosophy

Six things we hold ourselves to

1

Self Improvement

Every project teaches something. We train, keep our licences and methods current, and aim to do the next job better than the last.

2

Work Integrity

Quoted scope is delivered scope. Variations, substitutions and findings go in writing, and we tell you when the answer is inconvenient.

3

Health & Safety

Risk assessments and method statements before tools come out, permits where required, and the authority for anyone on site to stop unsafe work.

4

Teamwork

One crew across every discipline, working with your contractor, consultant and facilities team rather than around them.

5

Customer Service

Reachable people, honest timelines, and a report at the end that explains what was done and why.

6

Workmanship

Installed to the specification and the code, tested, documented, and built to be serviced years later.
