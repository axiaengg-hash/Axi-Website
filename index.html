Disciplines

MEPFS under one roof

Coordinating five trades through separate contractors is where programmes slip. We hold all five, so the services are designed, installed and tested together.

Mechanical

HVAC plant, air distribution, ducting, chilled and condenser water pipework, ventilation and extract systems.

Electrical

Distribution boards, power and lighting, containment, earthing, generators and structured cabling backbones.

Plumbing

Cold and hot water supply, booster pumps, drainage, storm water, tanks and distribution pipework.

Fire Protection

Sprinkler and hydrant systems, fire pumps, detection and alarm, suppression systems and fire-stopping.

Sanitary

Sanitary fixtures and fittings, waste and vent stacks, grease interceptors, and water treatment connections.

Structured Cabling

Copper and fibre backbones, horizontal cabling, patching, racks and cabinets, labelling and certified test results.
